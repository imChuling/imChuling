# Chuling Li

BSc Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence, University of Nottingham
Vocalist & keyboardist

## What I do

I build tools that give musicians more control over sound and creative expression. My work sits between music information retrieval, audio DSP, and human-computer interaction.

## Research

- **Quill** — Learning timbre and performance behavior as separate, swappable assets. Accepted at ISMIR 2026 LBD.
- **Pocket Producer** — Session-conditioned retrieval as user-controlled proposal. Accepted at ISMIR 2026 LBD.

## Music

I sing and play keys in a band I co-founded in 2023. We started in hard rock and blues, now stretching into R&B and beyond. I also build sound synthesis patches in Max/MSP and RNBO.
